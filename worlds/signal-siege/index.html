    How Signal Siege works

    1. Wire up your lymph node. A cell only works when it receives the signal it needs. Tap any cell to see what it's missing.
    2. Train an army. Neutrophils are fast and cheap. Macrophages are tough. Killer T cells destroy infected cells.
    3. Raid an infection. Tap the board to send cells in. Bacteria use quorum sensing: destroy a quorum hub and the toxin colonies that hear it go silent.
    4. Earn stars. One for the quorum hubs, one for 50% destroyed, one for clearing it all. Watch inflammation: too much at once causes a cytokine storm.
